---
name: Money Sense design direction
description: Standing audience and design-reference intent for Money Sense.
---

Money Sense is for everyone, primarily educated and concerned readers. Balance
technology-forward polish with familiar, mass-accessible controls.

**Why:** The user explicitly rejected the earlier designs and requested a fresh
system inspired by CRED, NerdWallet, Credit Karma and The Daily Star, not a clone.

**How to apply:** Preserve the supplied identity and publisher connection, avoid
exclusive-club messaging and news-portal density, and seek approval before
applying a newly presented system to existing screens.
