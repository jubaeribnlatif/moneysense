---
name: Money Sense sponsored placement boundary
description: Independence constraints for paid visibility, card matching, and editorial guidance.
---

Sponsored placement can buy clearly labelled visibility, not preferential suitability or editorial judgment.

**Why:** The user requires a credible, audience-first experience despite sponsorship.

**How to apply:** Keep payment status out of matching, scoring, result order, comparisons, and independent guidance; label paid placements clearly wherever they appear.
