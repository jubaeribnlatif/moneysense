# Research and direction

Reviewed public homepages on 2026-11-21. This is a fresh, generated system with
the stock component families themed as a whole, not a source-backed inventory.
No competitor images or page layouts are copied.

| Source | Observation | Decision for Money Sense |
|---|---|---|
| https://www.thedailystar.net/ | Serif-led editorial hierarchy, fine rules, monochrome structure, dense news taxonomy | Keep editorial contrast and publisher recognition; avoid a news-portal layout |
| https://cred.club/ | Confident dark composition, large serif display, product storytelling, exclusive membership message | Borrow scale and visual confidence, not exclusivity or long cinematic interactions |
| https://www.nerdwallet.com/ | Clear task taxonomy, prominent discovery, product guidance | Make discovery usable on arrival; retain an independent Money Sense identity |
| https://www.creditkarma.com/ | Everyday scenarios, benefit-led copy, personalised product paths | Explain relevance in plain language; never invent approval odds |

## Evidence and limitations
All four sites were fetched and their rendered homepages visually reviewed.
The Daily Star branding extraction reported Denton Condensed Test and IBM Plex
Serif, with 4px spacing and near-square controls. Its structured colour output
conflicted with its rendered page (orange/generic values versus visible
black/white/navy), so those inferred colours were rejected. The user's
black/white/green instruction overrides publisher-site accent colours.

The supplied PNG is retained unchanged under logos/ and in public/. The
signature accent is sampled from the supplied mark (dominant green near
`#19906D`). A tonal scale is preferable to multiple competing brand accents;
the logo's own pixels remain unchanged.

## Assets and typography
- Logo provenance: user-supplied Money Sense logo, previously staged in the
  mockup sandbox. Retained original raster, no redraw or recolouring.
- Instrument Serif: Google Fonts, temporary display substitute.
- DM Sans: Google Fonts, body/UI.
- Geist Mono: Google Fonts, financial labels and numeric specimens.
- Denton Test OTFs: excluded from this package pending licensed production
  Denton Condensed files. Substitution is explicit, not a brand redefinition.

## Product decisions
Audience: everyone, primarily educated and concerned readers. Banking products
come first; merchants support deal discovery. Build credibility through visible
fees, eligibility, terms, evidence dates and explainable matching—not abstract
trust badges. The supplied homepage sequence is retained in SKILL.md.

The current website mockups remain unchanged. Applying this system requires
separate user approval and re-homing in the mockup sandbox's design-system entry.
